**The Villages by CA network is taking part in the Sommet de l’Élevage (a major livestock trade show), held from 3 to 5 October in Clermont-Ferrand, in partnership with 11 Crédit Agricole regional banks\*, the Fédération Crédit Agricole Auvergne Rhône Alpes, Crédit Agricole S.A.'s Agriculture division and Pacifica. Over 3 days, start-ups from the Villages by CA network will present livestock professionals with innovations dedicated to their sector.**

Around a dozen start-ups from the Villages by CA network will be there to meet and talk with livestock professionals: Idis Group, [Janny MT](http://www.jannymt.com/), [Afyren](http://afyren.com/), [Copeeks](http://copeeks.fr/fr/), [Agrilend](https://agrilend.fr/), [Tell-élevage](https://www.tell-elevage.com/), [VotreMachine.com](https://votremachine.com/), [Ekylibre](/), [Applifarm](https://www.applifarm.io/), [GKEEP](http://www.g-keep.com/), [Stellapps](http://www.stellapps.com/), [Sencrop](https://sencrop.com/), [Karnott](https://www.karnott.fr/), [Ingenious Things](https://www.ingeniousthings.fr/).

*\*Centre France, Charente Périgord, Centre Ouest, Centre Loire, Centre Est, Franche Comté, Languedoc, Loire Haute Loire, Nord Midi Pyrénées, Sud Rhône Alpes and the Caisse Régionale des Savoie*
