*This article was originally published in French on the former ekylibre.com website.*

After 5 years in existence, and with as much passion as ever for what we build every day for farmers, it was time for us to breathe new life into our visual identity. We are delighted to unveil our new brand guidelines and the brand-new offers developed by Ekylibre!

### New offers?

Yes! But don't panic: nothing changes for our current users apart from the names of our various farm management tools. As you know, for more than a year we have been developing software dedicated to managing wine estates.\
This "**Eky**viti" offer is the very first Ekylibre solution dedicated to a specific agricultural sector!

We won't go into the details, but technically, being able to adapt our tools to the specific needs of each farming trade is a major step forward for us. We call it verticalisation!

### Ekyviti today… Ekyaqua tomorrow?

Why not! The principle behind the verticalisation of Ekylibre's offers is simple: each dedicated software package will carry (after the root "EKY") the first 2 syllables of its production sector.\
Today, we offer 2 dedicated solutions: **Eky**viti for winegrowers and **Eky**agri for farmers such as cereal growers, mixed crop-livestock farmers, market gardeners, fruit growers, beekeepers…

And tomorrow **Eky**arbo? **Eky**ani? **Eky**api? We'll keep you posted 😉

### Everything you need to know about Ekylibre's offers!

As you will have gathered, the Ekylibre software (which shares its name with our young company) is becoming **Eky**agri! For greater clarity, from today the name Ekylibre will refer only to the company, a publisher of Open Source software dedicated to the farming trades. With **Eky**agri, you can now manage your entire farm with a single tool!\
[See all of **Eky**agri's features!](/ekyagri/)

Ekyviti is the all-in-one management software dedicated to producers in the wine and spirits sector. With Ekyviti you manage:\
– all of your vineyard production\
– the automatic exchange of your data with your partner tools (Weenat, Samsys, Baqio, Fruition Sciences, Centipède…)\
– the environmental certification and sustainability of your vineyard\
– grape intake at harvest, by chatting with Duke!\
[See all of **Eky**viti's features!](/ekyviti/)
