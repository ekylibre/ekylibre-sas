[LFDay](http://lfday.fr/): this day, a first in France, bringing together large corporations, investors, start-ups (target: 50!) and academics, aims to promote AgTech momentum worldwide and to share thinking on the many challenges facing agriculture today and tomorrow: economic, social, societal and environmental.

**The ambition of this UN-PRE-CE-DENT-ED day!**

On this day dedicated to AgTech, we will feast on keynotes, round tables and start-up pitches, all to whet our appetite for exchange and innovation.\
[LFDay](http://lfday.fr/), this agricultural melting pot, will help ideas emerge and create synergies between large corporations, start-ups, schools, research institutes, investors, distributors, farmers…!\
Innovation will open the door to more efficient and more sustainable agriculture… We will build it and live it together!

**An event by La Ferme Digitale**

You know the association, but let's recap! [La Ferme Digitale](http://www.lafermedigitale.fr/) (a French non-profit association under the 1901 law) currently brings together 13 agricultural start-ups and focuses its actions on 2 main areas:\
– Creating a community of players who want to advance the agricultural sector through research and innovation\
– Bringing consumers and farmers closer together through digital technologies\
The promise? *Promoting innovation and digital technology for efficient and sustainable agriculture!*

**Our partner: NUMA Paris**

[NUMA](https://paris.numa.co/) supports entrepreneurs and innovators with a wide range of services: start-up acceleration programmes, open innovation projects, intrapreneurship and training for large corporations, and free events and coworking spaces for our communities.

A gathering of our whole ecosystem that you really shouldn't miss!\
Make Agriculture Great Again Forever 😉 #MAGF
