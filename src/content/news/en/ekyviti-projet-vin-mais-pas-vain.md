*This article was originally published in French on the former ekylibre.com website.*

Ekylibristes and pilot winegrowers met for the first time in Cognac, at the premises of the [syndicat des Producteurs et de Promotion des Vins de Pays Charentais](https://vindepayscharentais.fr/) (the association of producers and promoters of Charente country wines), to launch the test phase of [Ekyviti](https://ekyviti.fr/)!

Adapted from our Ekylibre software and developed specifically for the wine trades, Ekyviti will allow its users to manage their business from vine to cellar.

On the agenda for this first meeting, held in collaboration with [Novagency](https://www.novagency.eu/): getting to know the various stakeholders, launching the project and defining the roadmap.

With work getting under way at this meeting, two years of co-development between the field and the development team are now beginning. See you in 2021 for the completion of the project!
