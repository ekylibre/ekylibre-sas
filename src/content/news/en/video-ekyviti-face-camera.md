\[Report\] The team from Réussir (a French agricultural media group) produced this report on the estate of Florian Fossat (GAEC Fossat), based in Camiac-et-Saint-Denis (Gironde) in the heart of the Entre-deux-Mers appellation. Developed by our start-up project team, Ekyviti is an all-in-one management software package dedicated to producers in the wine and spirits sector. Frédéric Auriol and Florian Fossat take you into the vineyards! Watch them on video!

[Watch the video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRl1JpFo3cs)

For winegrowers and wine & spirits producers, [**Eky**viti](/ekyviti/) makes it possible to manage production and the business from one single interface.\
All the software solutions developed by [the Ekylibre company](/about/) aim to simplify farmers' daily lives by promising, above all, real time savings and a very precise, real-time view of everything happening across the business: production, stock, accounting, cash flow, suppliers, sales, teams, etc. This enables faster and better-informed decision-making.

As a farmer, equipping yourself with a tool such as **Eky**viti brings a certain peace of mind when it comes to day-to-day operations, regulatory procedures and keeping track of certifications.

> "*It's true that in farming we're always in a bit of a rush, always a bit behind with the work. Using such a comprehensive solution lets you step back and get an analysis that wasn't possible without software."*
>
> *Florian FOSSAT, winegrower*

Thanks to the development of interconnections, and through our partnership with the company [Samsys](https://samsys.fr/), we have been able to build into our solution the ability to geolocate all motorised work very precisely.\
We track very precisely the route of machines fitted with a box, and the passage of implements along the rows. At a glance, you can see whether a row has been missed!

With the same aim of simplifying farmers' daily lives, we have built a conversational assistant called Duke into our solutions. Talk to it and it records the information in the software for you.

> *"It's not a gadget, because it gets rid of paper in the tractors! \[…\] It takes me 5 minutes to record what each of my employees or contractors does on my farm."*
>
> *F.F.*
