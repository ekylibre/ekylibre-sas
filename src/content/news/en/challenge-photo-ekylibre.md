The Ekylibre Challenge is on!

Do you work in agriculture? You can take part in the **Ekylibre photo contest**, from 6 January to 6 February 2020!

All you need is a camera and your imagination: capture what, for you, makes your profession unique, all on the **theme of balance**.

There are plenty of prizes to be won: Ekylibre goodies, power banks, membership of the FranceAgriTwittos association, Agroéconomicus and Agronuméricus, books by Hervé Pillaud… And also **2 tickets to the Paris International Agricultural Show (SIA)**! (From 22 February to 3 March 2020) All the photos we receive will be hung on our Photowall, and every winner will also receive a signed photo of the Ekylibristes ?

Send your shots (up to 3 entries per person) to communication@ekylibre.com, starting the subject line with \[Challenge Ekylibre\]

Prizes can be collected at SIA 2020, on the Ekylibre stand.

**We can't wait to see your photos!!**
