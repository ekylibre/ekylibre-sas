Are you a **winegrower** or a **wine merchant**? Would you like a unified view of all the activities of your wine estate, or to manage your customer relationships efficiently?

Join this online event organised by Microsoft and discover the 3 solutions that will help you meet all your challenges, from vine to bottle: **Eky**viti, SpirIT and Cap Vignes.

### Join us on Thursday 4 February 2021 at 10 am – online event –

[Register for the webinar](https://forms.gle/yZBAQu4G2JFuggCi8)

Ekylibre recently launched **Eky**viti, an "all-in-one" management solution for your wine-growing and winemaking activities. In particular, it offers partner connections to collect data from connected devices, decision support tools (DSTs) or complementary management solutions. Its conversational assistant – Duke – helps you record all geolocated field operations or incidents by voice. [More information on its dedicated web page 😉](/ekyviti/)

The **Cap vignes** tool developed by **Cap’Vision** helps you save time and simplify your procedures by becoming the go-to management tool for your wine estate. A wine ERP designed for wineries, cooperatives, merchants and wine estates (production, logistics, stock management…). Find out more [here](https://cap-vignes.vin/).

**SpiriT** is a business solution from **Technomade** that helps you boost sales efficiency by equipping your teams with dedicated tools, strengthen your knowledge of your direct and indirect customers, and develop wine tourism by managing and following up your events. Find out more [here](https://www.technomade.com/wine-spirit-crm/).

- Presentation of Cap Vignes – by Cap Vision
