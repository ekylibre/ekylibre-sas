## What we set

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| `eky_consent` | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months | As soon as you make a choice |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4 audience measurement | 13 months at most | Only if you accept audience measurement |

No advertising cookies, no social network cookies. Until you accept audience measurement, no Google script is loaded.

## Changing your choices

Use the “Manage cookies” link in the footer: you can accept or reject audience measurement at any time. Rejecting has no effect on how you can use the site.
